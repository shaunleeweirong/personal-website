How to Use LinkedIn to Get
Your Brand Cited by AI

AI is reshaping how B2B buyers research products and build shortlists. Here is how to make your brand more discoverable and credible.

What gets cited on LinkedIn?
60%
of LinkedIn citations come from articles & posts
95%
come from original content, not reshares
75%
come from individual members, not Company Pages
1
Teach something useful
Educational content is your crown jewel
  • Answer questions your customers actually ask
  • Share firsthand expertise, original data or unique examples
  • Give specific steps, frameworks & recommendations
  • Prioritize useful info over generic opinions
Articles: 800–1,200 words
Posts: 200–300 words
2
Structure it for AI
Make answers easy to understand & cite
  • Put the main takeaway in the first line
  • Use a clear “Question : Answer” format
  • Define key terms & use relevant keywords
  • Organize advice into steps, lists or best practices
  • Add dates to signal freshness
  • Include a short TL;DR for longer content
3
Build author credibility
Credibility matters more than virality
  • Let subject-matter experts publish the original insight
  • Amplify it through executives & the Company Page
  • Publish 2–3× per week, or at least weekly
  • Encourage meaningful discussions & comments
  • Add descriptive captions to video content
  • Aim for 5M+ monthly impressions per thought leader (Thought Leader Ads)
The new goal is not just visibility.
It is buyability.
Expertise + Authority + Professional Trust
